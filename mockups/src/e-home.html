Our mission

We buy what the budget won't.

The Friends of Hendricks is a group of parents, teachers and volunteers that raises funds for resources and projects the Duval County School Board budget does not cover.

In the past we have funded faculty positions, capital projects, playground equipment, educational software, teacher grants and classroom supplies.
