Business Partner Program · 2026–27

Step into the spotlight as a Walkathon partner.

Partnering with the Friends of Hendricks Walkathon is more than a sponsorship. It puts your business in front of hundreds of San Marco families while paying for things a county budget will not.

See the five levels Talk to Aryn →

“With every step our students take, your business will be recognized as a leader walking alongside them.”

Business partner perks

Your brand, front and center across a whole school year.

Event signage

Yard signs with your logo at both school entrances, and your name on the Walkathon banner.

T-shirts

Every student wears one. Shirts are designed by a Hendricks student each year — last year by Cayla, Grade 4.

The school marquee

Your company thanked by name on the marquee for up to five days.

Digital

Up to five social posts and a logo on this site for the full year.

Five levels

What you get, at every level.

Sponsorship benefits our students and school while helping your business achieve greater recognition.
