Rent the HAE Spirit Rock.

The Friends of Hendricks manage the two School Spirit Rocks. Reserve one for 24 hours and paint it — congratulate a student, wish someone a happy birthday, cheer on a classmate, or thank a teacher who earned it.

  • $35one rock, front or back, 24 hours
  • $60both rocks, front and back, 24 hours

There are two: the front rock on the Hendricks Avenue side, and the back rock on the San Jose Boulevard side. Booking and payment both happen on SignUpGenius, where you pick your date and your rock and agree to the terms of use. Payment is due at the time of reservation.

You paint it yourself. Renters are responsible for painting the rock; painting services are not provided as part of the reservation.

Spirit Nights.

Turn family dinner into school support. Dine at a participating restaurant on the night, and a portion of total sales goes straight to Hendricks Avenue Elementary.

You get a break from cooking, the kids get to see their classmates outside school, and the school benefits with every bite. Tell them you are there for Hendricks Avenue Elementary.
