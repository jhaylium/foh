Your sponsorship can be credited to a student

Sponsorship dollars will be credited to a student of your choice — child, grandchild, niece or nephew — helping them reach their goal for the Walkathon incentive parties. Tell us the relationship when you get in touch and we will make sure the credit lands in the right place.

The 2027 Walkathon is Saturday, February 20th. Sponsorship is tax deductible — 501(c)(3), Tax ID 20-4931978.
