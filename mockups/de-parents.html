About The Friends of Hendricks

Parents. Volunteers. Unpaid.

The Friends of Hendricks is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization led by parent volunteers. It was created to provide funding so that the legacy of success stands, and the exemplary education of the students at Hendricks Avenue Elementary continues for years to come.

Mission statement

To increase the available funding to provide faculty and staff the additional resources required to ensure an exemplary education for the students of Hendricks Avenue Elementary School.

Choosing to strengthen Hendricks Avenue Elementary through a gift to The Friends of Hendricks is a valuable and meaningful decision. Support can be given through time, talents, and monetary and in-kind donations. The effect of parents, alumni, grandparents and friends standing together for our school will be immeasurable. All gifts, no matter how large or small, will have a powerful impact, because you become invested in the education and enrichment of every child, faculty and staff member.

The funds we raise are awarded through a grant process and used to offset county-implemented budget cuts and increase financial resources for school enhancements and improvements — staffing, technology and classroom needs.
